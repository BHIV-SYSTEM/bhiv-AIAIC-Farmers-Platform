## No account, no sign-in

Annapoorna has no sign-in and asks for no account. It does not ask for your name, phone number, PAN or bank details on its advice or selling screens connected to Krishi Sampada (AIAIC).

## What is sent where, and why

- **Advice:** the state, district, crop and other choices you make are sent from Annapoorna's server to Krishi Sampada (AIAIC)'s advice service, to answer you. AIAIC keeps a record of each answer it gives, which holds no name or phone number.
- **Plant check:** your photo is sent from Annapoorna's server to AIAIC's plant service for the check. It is not stored: AIAIC keeps a plant photo only when a signed-in person separately agrees, and Annapoorna has no sign-in.
- **Read aloud:** the text of the advice is sent to a voice service (BHIV Vaani) to be read out, or read by your own browser. That text is the advice, not your personal data.
- **"Was this useful?":** your answer is kept on your device and, where the team's feedback store is connected, sent to it with the topic, the language, the district, the crop, the mandi, the recommendation and the answer's reference. No name or phone number is sent.
- **Transport cost:** only the district, the mandi and the weight are sent to AIAIC's transport estimate; AIAIC may ask Geoapify, a routing service, for the road distance between those two places.
- **Fonts:** your browser loads the app's fonts from Google Fonts, whose servers see your device's internet address, as with any website.

## Practice selling

The selling screens are practice only. When Annapoorna is connected to AIAIC's sale service, they use AIAIC's practice account, and the listings, offers and orders are AIAIC's rehearsal records. When it is not connected, the app's own demonstration store keeps practice listings and orders on Annapoorna's server, including a name and a phone number if they are typed in its form. Please do not type real personal details there.

## On your device

The app keeps in your browser: your language, your saved farm (state, district, crop, mandi, land size and chosen topics), recent answers, your "Was this useful?" answers and your last plant check result. You can remove them by clearing this site's data in your browser. The app uses no advertising or analytics service.

## Who we share data with

Only as described above: Krishi Sampada (AIAIC), the BHIV Vaani voice service, the team's feedback store, Geoapify (through AIAIC) and Google Fonts; and authorities, when the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

## Your rights

To ask what data we hold about you, to correct or erase it, or to complain, write to the grievance officer named above.
