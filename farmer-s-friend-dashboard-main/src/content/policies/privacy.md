## As a guest, or with a farmer account

You can use Annapoorna as a guest, without an account. You can also register a farmer account with Krishi Sampada (AIAIC) from the app.

- **As a guest:** Annapoorna's server gives your phone a device id, kept in a cookie (`aiaic_guest`). It is made of random characters and holds no name or number. It goes with your advice, storage, transport and help requests to AIAIC, so that AIAIC's team can see what was asked from one device, and so that your help questions stay with your phone. It is kept up to 400 days, or until you clear this site's data in your browser.
- **Registering:** the app sends AIAIC your username, your password, your name if you give it, your state and district, your language and your agreement to the registration notice. AIAIC keeps your password only as a bcrypt hash, never in readable form; neither Annapoorna nor AIAIC writes it in a log. AIAIC's team approves or refuses each registration, and the app tells you when your account is approved.
- **Signing in:** AIAIC's sign-in token is kept in a cookie (`aiaic_session`) that only Annapoorna's server reads; the page's own scripts cannot read it. It ends when the token ends (12 hours, unless AIAIC's operator sets another period) or when you sign out; when AIAIC ends it first, a third cookie (`aiaic_note`) keeps for one day that the app should tell you so. While you are signed in, your requests go to AIAIC with your account instead of the device id. Registering or signing in never joins what was asked as a guest to your account.
- The app asks for no phone number or Aadhaar number. PAN and bank or UPI details are asked only on the payout form described under Selling.

## What is sent where, and why

- **Advice:** the state, district, crop and other choices you make are sent from Annapoorna's server to Krishi Sampada (AIAIC)'s advice service, to answer you. AIAIC keeps a record of each answer it gives, under your account when you are signed in, else under the device id. The record holds no name or phone number.
- **Storage and transport:** the storage card sends AIAIC the state, the district, the crop and the quantity; the transport cost sends the state and district, the mandi and its place, and the weight. AIAIC keeps a short note of each (the warehouse or mandi, the distance and the main figures of its answer) under your account or the device id. For the road distance, AIAIC may ask Geoapify, a routing service, with only the two places.
- **Plant check:** your photo is sent from Annapoorna's server to AIAIC's plant service for the check, without your account or the device id. It is not stored: AIAIC keeps a plant photo only when a signed-in person separately agrees, and Annapoorna never asks it to.
- **Read aloud:** the text of the advice is sent to a voice service (BHIV Vaani) to be read out, or read by your own browser. That text is the advice, not your personal data.
- **"Was this useful?":** your answer is kept on your device and, where the team's feedback store is connected, sent to it with the topic, the language, the district, the crop, the mandi, the recommendation and the answer's reference. No name or phone number is sent.
- **Fonts:** your browser loads the app's fonts from Google Fonts, whose servers see your device's internet address, as with any website.

## Help questions

When you ask AIAIC's team a question on the Help tab, the app sends AIAIC its topic, your words, your saved farm's state, district and crop, and your language, with your account when you are signed in, else with the device id. AIAIC's team (its admin and reviewer accounts) reads it in AIAIC's dashboard, beside what was asked from your account or device, and answers; the answers appear on the Help tab. If what you write reads as distress, the app first shows where to find help, and that message is not kept. Please do not write a bank account, Aadhaar or PAN number in a question.

## Selling

- **As a guest,** the selling screens are practice only. When Annapoorna is connected to AIAIC's sale service, they use AIAIC's practice account, and the listings, offers and orders are AIAIC's rehearsal records. When it is not connected, the app's own demonstration store keeps practice listings and orders on Annapoorna's server, including a name and a phone number if they are typed in its form. Please do not type real personal details there.
- **Signed in,** the selling screens show and change your own records on AIAIC: your listings, the offers made to you and the orders you accept are kept by AIAIC under your account, and AIAIC's team sees them.
- **The payout form** on the Payments screen appears only where AIAIC offers it. The PAN and bank or UPI details typed in it pass through Annapoorna's server to AIAIC and on to Razorpay, a payment service; Annapoorna keeps none of them. While AIAIC's payments run in test mode, it is offered only to the practice account, and only test values belong in it.

## How long records are kept

AIAIC keeps the records of its answers, the notes of storage and transport looks and the help questions until its operator's retention period ends, where the operator has set one. To have your account erased, write to the grievance officer named above: your help questions and the notes of your looks are removed with it, the records of AIAIC's answers are kept without your account, and your sale records stay as records of the sale without your name. A guest's records are tied only to the device id; once you clear this site's data, nothing on your phone ties them to you any more.

## On your device

The app keeps in your browser: your language, your saved farm (state, district, crop, mandi, land size and chosen topics), recent answers, your "Was this useful?" answers and your last plant check result, and the cookies named above. You can remove them by clearing this site's data in your browser. The app uses no advertising or analytics service.

## Who we share data with

Only as described above: Krishi Sampada (AIAIC) and its team, the BHIV Vaani voice service, the team's feedback store, Geoapify and Razorpay (both through AIAIC) and Google Fonts; and authorities, when the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

## Your rights

To ask what data we hold about you, to correct or erase it, or to complain, write to the grievance officer named above.
