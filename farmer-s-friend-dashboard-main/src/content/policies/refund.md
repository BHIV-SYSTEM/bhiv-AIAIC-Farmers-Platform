# Cancellation & Refund Policy

_Last updated: {{last_updated}}_

## Annapoorna takes no payment

Annapoorna is free and takes no payment, so there is nothing to refund for using it.

## Practice orders

The selling screens are practice only. Their orders are rehearsal records: no money is taken or paid, so there is nothing to cancel for money and nothing to refund.

## Real sales, on Krishi Sampada (AIAIC)

Real sales and their payments are made on Krishi Sampada (AIAIC), and AIAIC's Cancellation & Refund Policy applies to them. In short:

- An order can be cancelled until the produce leaves the farm (a pickup recorded as done). AIAIC sends the farmer their amount only after the produce has left the farm, so until then the buyer's payment is still with AIAIC. Once the produce has left the farm, a problem is recorded as a delivery problem and handled as a dispute by AIAIC's team.
- If an order is cancelled after the buyer paid, the full amount paid is owed back to the buyer, and AIAIC's team makes the refund through Cashfree, AIAIC's payment processor, for the payment the buyer made on the order's payment link.
- Cashfree states that a refund can take 7 to 10 business days to reach the buyer's account, depending on the payment method.
- AIAIC never takes back money that has reached the farmer: a refund agreed for the buyer after that is paid from AIAIC's own funds.

The full policy is on Krishi Sampada (AIAIC) at [krishisampada.blackholeinfiverse.com](https://krishisampada.blackholeinfiverse.com/).
