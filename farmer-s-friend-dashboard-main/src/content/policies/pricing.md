# Pricing Details

_Last updated: {{last_updated}}_

## Annapoorna is free

The advice, the plant check, reading aloud and the transport estimates are free. There is no subscription, no sign-up and no fee. Annapoorna takes no payment.

## Selling: practice only, for now

The selling screens (Sell, My Listings, Orders & Offers, Payments) are practice only. They run on Krishi Sampada (AIAIC)'s sale service through a practice account or, where Annapoorna is not connected to it, on the app's own demonstration store. Either way, the listings, offers, orders and amounts there are rehearsal records: no money is taken, and nothing is paid to anyone.

## What a real sale costs, on Krishi Sampada (AIAIC)

Real sales are made on Krishi Sampada (AIAIC), and AIAIC's Pricing Details apply. In short:

- **Produce value** = quantity (quintals) × the price per quintal the farmer accepted.
- **Payment fee:** the buyer pays the payment fee on top, AIAIC's estimate of the charge of Cashfree, AIAIC's payment processor, with GST on it; it never comes out of the farmer's price, and if Cashfree's real fee differs, the difference is AIAIC's.
- **AIAIC's commission:** {{commission_sentence}}
- **AIAIC collects, then pays you:** the buyer pays AIAIC's Cashfree payment link for the buyer's total (the agreed price, the buyer's part of AIAIC's commission and the GST on it, and the payment fee); AIAIC then sends you the agreed price less your part of the commission and the GST on that part. You pay no payment fee. Your sale's screen shows that amount before anything happens, and each step after (the buyer pays, AIAIC sends, it reaches your bank). A payment to a UPI ID is kept below ₹1,00,000, so a larger amount goes to a bank account.

## How the money moves in a real sale

- **The link:** the farmer accepts a buyer's offer, and AIAIC makes a Cashfree payment link for the order: the full amount in one payment, valid for 7 days.
- **The buyer pays** on Cashfree's own page; card, UPI or bank details are typed there, never into AIAIC or Annapoorna. The payment reaches AIAIC's account at Cashfree, and AIAIC marks the order paid only when Cashfree confirms the payment.
- **AIAIC pays the farmer:** once the order is paid in full and the produce has left the farmer, AIAIC's team sends the farmer their amount through Cashfree Payouts to their own bank account or UPI ID (or, where Cashfree Payouts is not available, by a bank transfer the team makes and records with its bank reference). Annapoorna says it reached your bank only on Cashfree's word.
- **What AIAIC keeps:** its commission and the GST on it (owed to the government); the payment fee pays Cashfree's charge.

AIAIC is neither the buyer nor the seller: it brings them together, records the order, makes the payment link and sends the farmer their amount from the buyer's payment. Annapoorna's Payments screen only shows where a payment stands.
